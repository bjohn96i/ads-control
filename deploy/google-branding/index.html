ADS-Control

ADS-Control is a private dashboard for one household. It runs on a computer in the home and shows the house's energy use, maintenance list, bills, thermostats and cameras to the people who live there.

It is not a public service: there are no accounts to sign up for, and it is only reachable on the home network. It connects to Google Nest so the household can see its own thermostats and camera events, and change a thermostat when someone presses a button.
