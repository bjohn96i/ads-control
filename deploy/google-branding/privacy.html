ADS-Control privacy policy

Last updated November 4, 2026.

ADS-Control is a private dashboard used by a single household on its own home network. This policy explains what it does with information from Google.

What it accesses

  • Nest thermostats: temperature, humidity, mode and setpoint, and changes to the setpoint when a person in the household presses a button in the app. It never changes a schedule.
  • Nest cameras and doorbell: the list of cameras, event notices (motion, person, sound, doorbell press) with their time, and live video while someone is watching. Camera images and video are not stored.

How it is used and stored

This information is used only to show the household its own devices and to let it adjust its own thermostats. It is stored on the household's own computer, on the home network, and is not sent to anyone else. It is not sold, not shared, not used for advertising, and not used to train any model.

ADS-Control's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Access can be removed at any time from the Google Account's third-party access page. The stored readings and event notices can be deleted from the household's computer on request.
